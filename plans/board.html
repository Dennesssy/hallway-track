The idea

Hallway Track: a conference app that slows down, explains itself, and gets fixed in front of the judges.

Attendees find the right talk by meaning (Elasticsearch), build a time-zone-correct agenda, and find a quiet, step-free, captioned room. When 170 people open it at once after the keynote it really slows; Dash0 explains why, Radar shows the cluster coping, and Gitar-reviewed pull requests fix it. One story, every track.
